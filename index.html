Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding meal deals, combo prices, chicken buckets, and current promotions.

Current KFC menu prices generally start around Rs. 310 ($1.10) for Everyday Value Krunch Burgers and Rs. 620 ($2.25) for value combos. Legendary burgers like the Classic Zinger are priced around Rs. 650 ($2.35), while all-in-one Signature Boxes range from Rs. 1,050 to Rs. 1,650 ($3.75 - $5.95). Family sharing chicken buckets range from Rs. 2,450 to Rs. 3,890 ($8.80 - $13.90).

Our interactive directory provides a complete price list for KFC, featuring local meal rates and estimated international USD conversions for diners comparing the KFC USA menu with prices and global combo deals.

The individual KFC chicken sandwich cost for the Classic Zinger starts at Rs. 650 ($2.35) ala carte, while the KFC spicy chicken sandwich price for the full combo meal (with secret recipe fries and a drink) is approximately Rs. 950 ($3.40).

Kentucky Fried Chicken menu bucket prices range from Rs. 2,450 ($8.80) for the 9-piece value bucket to Rs. 4,750 ($16.99) for super feasts. The popular KFC 12 pc meal price is Rs. 3,890 ($13.90), bundling 12 chicken pieces, 3 large sides, and 6 warm dinner rolls or biscuits.

An individual warm KFC biscuit price starts at approximately Rs. 180 ($0.65), with 4-pack bundles around Rs. 650 ($2.35). The KFC chicken strips price for a 3-piece extra crispy tenders combo with dipping sauce, fries, and a fountain drink is Rs. 890 ($3.20).

KFC Everyday Value includes wallet-friendly individual meal options such as the Krunch Burger, Krunch Combo Meal (burger, fries, and drink), Rice & Spice bowl, Zingeratha paratha wrap, and Twister wraps.

KFC Signature Boxes are designed to offer a complete feast in one box. Popular options include the Crispy Box (1 piece chicken, fries, dinner roll with gravy, and drink for Rs. 1,050), Boneless Box (4 tenders, fries, roll, and drink for Rs. 1,150), WOW Box (Zinger burger, 1 piece chicken, fries, and drink for Rs. 1,250), and Xtreme Duo Box for two people.

KFC Midnight Deals are exclusive late-night discounted meal bundles that start every night at 12:00 AM (midnight) and usually run until 3:00 AM. They provide significant savings on Zinger combos, double Krunch meals, and snack boxes for night owls.

Yes, KFC prices can slightly differ between in-store dine-in/takeaway counter prices and third-party delivery services (like Foodpanda) due to platform commission rates and local delivery fees.
